# 🌾 KRISHINITI — Crops for Soil Health & High Profitability

A practical, easy-to-understand reference guide on crop options that build soil health (nitrogen fixation, aeration, pest management) while maximizing farm income.

## 2. Quick Farm Decision Guide (Table Matrix)

Find the right crop based on your immediate farm situation:

| If Your Situation Is... | Best Crop Option | Why This Works Best |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Short 60-day window between main seasons** | **Green Gram (Moong)** or **Cowpea (Lobia)** | Quick maturity, enriches soil with nitrogen before next main crop. |
| **Hard, compacted soil that restricts drainage** | **Pigeon Pea (Arhar)** or **Sunflower** | Deep taproot system pierces the hardpan naturally, loosening the soil. |
| **Scarcity of irrigation water** | **Mustard**, **Pigeon Pea**, or **Cowpea** | High drought tolerance; needs 60% less water than paddy or sugarcane. |
| **Soil infested with nematodes or root diseases** | **Mustard / Rapeseed** | Decomposing glucosinolates act as natural biofumigants. |
| **Need for immediate weekly cash flow** | **Fenugreek (Methi)** or **Cowpea (Green Pods)** | Quick vegetative growth allows weekly harvests for local markets. |
| **Need for high cash crop plus livestock fodder** | **Groundnut (Peanut)** | Sells for cooking oil and snacks; leaves/stems make protein-rich hay. |

## 3. Recommended Multi-Year Crop Rotation Plans

| Season Cycle | Rotation Pattern | Soil Health Impact | Economic Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Plan 1 (Cereal – Pulse – Oilseed)** | **Rice (Kharif) → Mustard (Rabi) → Moong (Summer)** | Balanced root depth, pest cycle break, 150+ kg/ha nitrogen credit from Moong. | 3 income cycles per year; cuts winter irrigation costs. |
| **Plan 2 (Intercropping Duo)** | **Cotton + Pigeon Pea (Arhar) (4:1 Row Ratio)** | Pigeon pea fixes nitrogen and mines subsoil water without competing with cotton. | Diversified income: cotton cash + high-value pulse buffer against market dips. |
| **Plan 3 (Soil Restorer Cycle)** | **Maize (Kharif) → Lentils / Chickpea (Rabi) → Cowpea Fodder (Summer)** | Continuous soil biological cover, prevents nutrient leaching, builds organic matter. | Reduced synthetic fertilizer spend; dual revenue from grain and fodder. |

## 4. 3 Golden Principles for Long-Term Soil Health

1. **Rotate Heavy Feeders with Restorative Crops:** Never plant cereal-after-cereal (like Rice after Rice or Wheat after Maize) continuously. Follow a heavy feeder with a legume (Moong, Lentils, Cowpea).
2. **Retain and Incorporate Residues:** Never burn crop stubble or haulms. Chopping and incorporating them into the topsoil provides carbon food for soil microbes and earthworms.
3. **Keep the Soil Covered:** Direct sun and heavy rain degrade bare topsoil. Growing short-duration legumes keeps soil cool, retains moisture, and stops weed emergence.
