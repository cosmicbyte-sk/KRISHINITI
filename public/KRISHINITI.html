What Should You Bring to the Laboratory?

                        

                        
    
                          
  • Representative Soil Sample: Collect ~500 grams of composite soil from 0–15 cm depth across 8–10 spots in a V-shape pattern. Avoid field edges, manure heaps, and stagnant water channels. Mix thoroughly and dry in shade before bagging.
    • 
                          
  • Farm Location Details: Khata / Khesra / Survey number, village, block, and district name.
    • 
                          
  • Farmer Identification Details: Aadhaar number or Kisan Credit Card (KCC) details (required by government laboratories for Soil Health Card subsidies).
    • 
                          
  • Previous Cropping History: Last two crop seasons and fertilizer/manure application records if available.
    • 
                          
  • Note: Every laboratory may have specific submission containers or nominal testing fees. We recommend calling the laboratory before visiting to confirm operating hours.

Water and Irrigation

                    
Select how water reaches your crops. Note: Selecting an irrigation source does not assume unlimited water availability.

                  

                


                

                  

                    
🌧️

                    Rainfed Farming
                    
Strictly dependent on natural monsoon rainfall. High sensitivity to prolonged dry spells.

                  

                  

                    
💧

                    Canal Irrigation
                    
Seasonal irrigation from canal releases or government lift schemes when water is scheduled.

                  

                  

                    
🚜

                    Borewell / Tubewell
                    
Groundwater pumping from private or shared borewell. Electricity and water table dependent.

                  

                  

                    
🚿

                    Drip Irrigation
                    
Precision micro-irrigation saving 35–50% water with pressurized root-zone delivery.

                  

                  

                    
💦

                    Sprinkler Irrigation
                    
Overhead sprinkler nozzles suitable for pulses, mustard, and undulating terrain.

                  

                  

                    
🔄

                    Multiple Sources
                    
Combination of seasonal canal, groundwater tubewell, and farm pond storage.
